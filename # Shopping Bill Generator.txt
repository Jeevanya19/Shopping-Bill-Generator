# Shopping Bill Generator

## Description

A simple Python program that accepts product names, quantities, and prices and generates a formatted shopping bill.

## Objective

To practice lists, dictionaries, loops, functions, input validation, and basic calculations.

##

## Sample Output

```text
===== SHOPPING BILL GENERATOR =====

Enter product name (or 'done' to finish): Notebook
Enter quantity: 2
Enter price per item: 100

Enter product name (or 'done' to finish): Pen
Enter quantity: 5
Enter price per item: 20

Enter product name (or 'done' to finish): done

========== SHOPPING BILL ==========
Product        Qty   Price     Total
-----------------------------------
Notebook       2     100.00    200.00
Pen            5     20.00     100.00
-----------------------------------
Subtotal:         Rs. 300.00
Discount:         Rs. 0.00
Tax (5%):         Rs. 15.00
Final Amount:     Rs. 315.00
===================================
```
